How to use

  • Pick a place. The dashed circle is only a guide to how big it should be for its population. The markers round the edge show which way roads arrive and roughly where the water is.
  • Water: circle an area for a lake or bay. For a coast, draw the shoreline as one open line: the sea fills outwards from it, on the side away from the centre. For rivers use the River line (about 45 m wide) or the Wide river line (about 250 m) with Draw.
  • Clear area: circle an area to remove every street in it. Clear streets in water (in the Clean up menu) trims streets and lanes that lie under water or under a river. Avenues are kept: an avenue over water is a bridge.
  • Pattern: circle an area, choose a pattern, block size and angle, then Fill. Mix patterns by filling different areas.
  • Patterns join to streets already beside them when you fill. Join loose ends does the same for the whole plan.
  • Draw: add streets by hand. Ends snap to other streets.
  • District: choose a district, then circle an area. One district can be drawn in several places. Tap a drawn district with this tool to select it, change it or delete it. A selected district shows a box: drag a corner square to resize it, or the round handle in the middle to move it. Buildings inside it go with it. Allotments shows how much of each is placed.
  • Building: tap inside a drawn district and pick one of its buildings to place there. In a residential area you also choose its main housing type. A selected building can be dragged to move it; drag a corner square to resize it or the round handle to turn it. A town's single pieces can be placed inside or outside a district. Villages have no districts: tap anywhere and choose from every building.
  • Shape: with Draw, choose Freehand, Straight line (drag from one end to the other) or Circle (press at the middle and drag out to the size you want). Works with every line type.
  • Whole-town layouts: the pattern list also has three starting layouts for towns and cities: grown out from its centre, stretched along its main road, and grown back from the water. Each has a small dense centre, a wide looser belt of housing streets, and then only a few roads carrying on outwards with side lanes that thin out. The roads head for the arriving roads. Use Fill whole place, then change what you don't like. For the water layout, turn the Angle to point at the water.
  • Village layouts: the pattern list ends with six small layouts for villages: one main street, crossroads, around a green, fork, cluster of lanes, and along the water. With Pattern chosen, Fill whole place uses the size-guide circle so you don't have to circle the village. Turn the Angle to point the main street the way you want.
  • Filling blocks: with the District tool, tap inside a block (ground closed in by streets, water or other districts) to fill it with the chosen district. Circling by hand still works.
  • Joining: a new district joins any district of the same kind it touches (or sits across one street from), and they become one area. Residential areas never join, so each keeps its own housing type. Turn this off in the View menu. Clean up has a button to join the ones already drawn.
  • Right-click or press and hold on a street, district, building or empty ground for a short menu: change its type, delete it, fill the block, or add a building.
  • To do lists what is still missing and jumps to it when tapped. The colour key at the right shows this place's districts; tap one to choose it.
  • Roundabout: tap where streets cross to put a roundabout there. Patterns can also add them where their avenues cross.
  • Walk rings: dotted rings show how many minutes on foot a spot is from the centre, at about 80 m a minute. The bar at the bottom shows the walk time for the line or area you are drawing.
  • Select: tap a street to change its type, delete it, or delete the whole pattern it came from. Tap a water area to delete it.
  • Erase: rub out part of a street.
  • Clear all empties the place so you can start over. Undo brings it back. Your allotment numbers are kept.
  • Stylus or mouse draws; fingers move and pinch. Mouse wheel zooms; hold Space or right-drag to move.
  • Save writes one file per place. The page also keeps a backup of each place in this browser.
  • Keys: 1–6 line type, D P W A B S E tools, Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y, Ctrl+S.
