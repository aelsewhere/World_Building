Known limits of this data (Nov 4)
  • Climate data is from the Pass 1 heights; Morasil's big southern island is now higher.
  • Hoakamotu's land use has colour-reading errors: stray salt flats and peat and tussock in the tropics; the northwest island group reads as salt flats but is recorded as Mediterranean scrub.
  • Not yet drawn: Mesomara's islet pair A as temperate forest; the Iskay Islands' Mediterranean scrub; Kamon's springs on Koshio and Sueshio; the Hoaka Sea loop and Gulf of Mor throughflow; Hoakamotu's mudflow valley.
  • Pass 3 checks still to do: where continents meet and across the map edge (Moramotu Sea); the three islets east of Elmiyaro's Byrland.
  • Snow is recorded for Morasil and Hoakamotu only.
